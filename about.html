About WAIS

Wise Assistance for Inclusive Undergraduate Students' Savings and Credit

Helping Students Succeed

WAIS Cooperative is a proposed student-centered cooperative designed to provide financial and educational support to undergraduate students in selected barangays of Upper Antipolo City.

Our mission is to help college students manage temporary financial difficulties and develop better financial habits through accessible savings programs, educational loans, and financial literacy activities.
