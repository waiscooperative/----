Frequently Asked Questions

How do I make a deposit to my savings account?

You can make deposits by visiting our office during business hours, or through our upcoming mobile app. Currently, walk-in deposits are accepted at our main office.

What are the requirements for a loan application?

To apply for a loan, you need to be an active member for at least 3 months, have a good payment history, and provide proof of enrollment. Loan amounts depend on your savings balance and repayment capacity.

How can I update my personal information?

You can update your personal information by visiting the "My Profile" section in your member portal, or by visiting our office with valid identification documents.

What happens if I miss a loan payment?

If you miss a payment, please contact us immediately. We can work with you to arrange a payment plan. Late payments may affect your ability to borrow in the future and may incur late fees.
