About Educational Loans

Short-term loans designed for school-related expenses

Program Overview

WAIS provides short-term educational loans to help students cover school-related expenses when temporary financial difficulties arise. Our loan program is designed with student-friendly terms and a straightforward application process.

The loan program is intended to support undergraduate students in managing academic expenses without disrupting their education.

Loan Purposes

  • Tuition and enrollment fees
  • Examination fees
  • Academic expenses
  • Other school-related expenses

Application Process

From inquiry to loan release

Inquiry

1. Inquiry Phase

Student recognizes need for financial assistance. Staff explains available services, rates, and requirements. Student decides whether to apply.

Application

2. Application Phase

Student requests application form, prepares documents, completes application form and documents, and submits required forms/documents.

Evaluation

3. Evaluation Phase

Staff verifies submitted documents, conducts credit investigation, evaluates member's capacity to pay, reviews credit investigation, and authorized officer/committee reviews application.

Release

4. Release Phase

If approved: Terms and conditions are explained, applicant accepts terms, loan agreement is prepared and signed, final verification is done, approved loan amount is released, transaction is recorded, and repayment schedule is provided.

Repayment

5. Repayment & Monitoring Phase

Student makes scheduled payment, staff records payment, loan account is updated and monitored. When fully paid, the account is closed.

Requirements

Documents needed for loan application

General Requirements

  • Valid student ID
  • Proof of enrollment
  • Completed loan application form
  • Proof of income or allowance
  • Government-issued ID
  • Recent passport-sized photos
  • Billing statement or proof of address

Additional requirements may be requested based on the loan amount and purpose.
