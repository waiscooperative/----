Eligibility criteria and documents needed

Membership Requirements

Documents needed to apply for WAIS membership

Required Documents

  • Completed membership application form
  • Valid government-issued ID (e.g., UMID, Passport, Driver's License)
  • Student ID from current educational institution
  • Proof of enrollment (certificate of registration or equivalent)
  • Proof of residence (barangay clearance or utility bill)
  • 2x2 recent passport-sized photos (2 copies)
  • Birth certificate (NSO/PSA copy)
  • Proof of income or allowance (if applicable)

Additional documents may be requested upon review of the application.

Savings Account Requirements

Documents needed to open a savings account

Prerequisites

  • Must be an active WAIS member in good standing
  • Must have completed membership verification process

Required Documents

  • Completed savings account application form
  • Valid member ID
  • Initial deposit amount (minimum to be determined)
  • Signature card

Educational Loan Requirements

Documents needed to apply for an educational loan

Prerequisites

  • Must be an active WAIS member in good standing
  • Must have active savings account (if applicable)
  • Must demonstrate capacity to repay

Required Documents

  • Completed loan application form
  • Valid member ID
  • Proof of enrollment (current semester)
  • Proof of income or allowance
  • Statement of loan purpose
  • Supporting documents for loan purpose (e.g., tuition fee assessment)
  • Government-issued ID
  • Recent passport-sized photo (1 copy)

Additional Requirements (if applicable)

  • Co-maker or guarantor information
  • Existing financial obligations disclosure
  • Parent/guardian consent (for minors)

Important Notes

Please read before applying

Document Authenticity

All submitted documents must be authentic and valid. Submission of falsified documents will result in automatic disqualification and possible legal action.

Document Validity

IDs and other documents with expiration dates must be valid at the time of application. Expired documents will not be accepted.

Processing Time

Application processing time varies depending on the completeness of requirements and the volume of applications. Applicants will be notified of the status.

Confidentiality

All personal and financial information submitted will be treated with confidentiality and used only for the purpose of application processing.
