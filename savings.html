About the Program

The WAIS Savings Program helps student members build financial discipline and save for important academic milestones. Our program is designed to be accessible, secure, and tailored to student needs.

Whether you're saving for your thesis, graduation fees, a new laptop, or building an emergency fund, WAIS provides a structured way to achieve your savings goals.

Key Benefits

  • Regular deposit tracking and monitoring
  • Set and track savings goals
  • Complete transaction history
  • Progress monitoring and updates
  • Secure account management
  • Accessible withdrawal options

Account Opening Process

From inquiry to active savings account

Inquiry

1. Inquiry Phase

Student expresses interest in opening a savings account. Staff explains the available savings program, rates, and requirements.

Application

2. Application Phase

Student requests application form, prepares documents, completes application, and submits required forms/documents.

Verification

3. Verification Phase

Staff checks submitted forms and requirements. If requirements are incomplete, student completes and submits missing requirements.

Account Opening

4. Account Opening Phase

Student makes initial deposit, staff records transaction, savings account is opened/activated, and student receives receipt/account record.

Monitoring

5. Savings Monitor Phase

Student makes future deposits, staff records transactions, account balance is updated, and staff monitors savings account.
