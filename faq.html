General Questions

Basic information about WAIS

What is WAIS Cooperative?

WAIS (Wise Assistance for Inclusive Undergraduate Students' Savings and Credit) is a proposed student-centered cooperative designed to provide financial and educational support to undergraduate students in selected barangays of Upper Antipolo City.

Who can join WAIS?

WAIS membership is open to undergraduate college students aged approximately 17-30 who reside in the target barangays: Dalig, San Jose, San Isidro, and San Roque in Upper Antipolo City.

Where is WAIS located?

The proposed location is Ground Floor E & H Building, Manuel L. Quezon Ext, Dalig, Antipolo, 1870 Rizal. This serves the target areas of Dalig, San Roque, San Jose, and Mahabang Parang.

Is WAIS currently operational?

No, WAIS is currently in the planning phase based on a feasibility study. The cooperative is not yet operational. This website represents the planned services and structure.

Membership Questions

Questions about joining WAIS

How do I apply for membership?

To apply for membership, you need to complete a membership application form and submit required documents including valid ID, student ID, proof of enrollment, proof of residence, and other documents as specified in the requirements page.

What are the benefits of being a member?

Members have access to the Savings Program, Educational Loans, Gadget Loans, Financial Literacy programs, voting rights in cooperative decisions, and other member benefits.

Is there a membership fee?

Membership fee details will be determined upon implementation. This information will be provided once the cooperative becomes operational.

How long does membership application take?

Processing time varies depending on the completeness of requirements and volume of applications. Applicants will be notified of their application status through the process.

Services Questions

Questions about WAIS services

What services does WAIS offer?

WAIS offers three main services: Savings Program, Educational Loans (including Micro Loans and Gadget Loans), and Financial Literacy Program. Each service is designed to support students' academic and financial needs.

What are the loan terms?

Based on the feasibility study, the proposed loan terms are: 3% monthly interest, ₱150 service/processing fee, and 3% late payment penalty. These terms are subject to finalization upon implementation.

What can I save for?

You can save for various academic goals including thesis, graduation fees, gadgets and study tools, future needs, emergency funds, or custom savings goals you set for yourself.

What gadgets are available for loan?

Gadget Loans will be available for laptops, tablets, printers, and other study devices with student-friendly interest rates and flexible repayment terms. Actual gadget availability will be determined upon operation.

Financial Questions

Questions about finances and security

Is my money safe with WAIS?

As a cooperative, WAIS will follow cooperative principles and regulations to ensure the security of members' funds. Specific security measures will be implemented upon operation.

How are loan repayments structured?

Loan repayments will follow a schedule provided upon loan approval. Repayment terms will be designed to be manageable for students while ensuring cooperative sustainability.

Can I withdraw from my savings anytime?

Savings withdrawal policies will be established upon implementation. The cooperative aims to provide reasonable access to savings while maintaining account stability.

What happens if I can't repay my loan on time?

Late payment penalties (proposed at 3%) may apply. Members facing repayment difficulties are encouraged to communicate with WAIS staff to explore possible arrangements.
