Membership

Join WAIS and start building your financial future

Why Become a Member?

Benefits of joining WAIS Cooperative

Member Benefits

  • Access to Savings Program
  • Educational Loan eligibility
  • Gadget Loans access
  • Financial Literacy programs
  • Voting rights in cooperative decisions
  • Patronage refunds (when applicable)
  • Community support network

Who Can Join?

WAIS membership is open to undergraduate college students from our target barangays in Upper Antipolo City.

Target Areas:

  • Dalig
  • San Roque
  • San Jose
  • Mahabang Parang

Applicants must be currently enrolled in an undergraduate program and meet the membership requirements.

Membership Application Process

Steps to become a WAIS member

1

Submit Application

Complete and submit the membership application form with required documents.

2

Document Verification

Staff reviews your application and verifies all submitted requirements.

3

Application Review

Your application is reviewed by the membership committee for approval.

4

Membership Approval

Upon approval, you become an official WAIS member and can access all services.

Member Responsibilities

What is expected of WAIS members

Financial Responsibilities

  • Pay membership fees (if applicable)
  • Honor loan repayment commitments
  • Maintain regular savings deposits
  • Update personal information promptly

Cooperative Responsibilities

  • Participate in general assemblies
  • Exercise voting rights responsibly
  • Support cooperative activities
  • Promote the cooperative's mission
