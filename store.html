About the Store

Quality academic essentials at cooperative prices

What We Offer

The WAIS Student Store offers essential academic tools and equipment at cooperative prices. Members can access affordable laptops, tablets, printers, and study essentials to support their educational needs.

Our store is designed to help students acquire the tools they need for their studies without the burden of high retail prices.

Store Benefits

  • Quality academic equipment

  • Order history tracking
  • Product availability updates
