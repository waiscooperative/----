Savings Program

Our savings program helps student members build financial discipline and save for important academic milestones. Whether you're saving for your thesis, graduation fees, a new laptop, or emergency needs, WAIS provides a secure and accessible way to grow your savings.

Savings Goals May Include:

  • Thesis and research expenses
  • Graduation fees and requirements
  • Gadgets and study tools
  • Future academic needs
  • Emergency funds

Educational Loans

WAIS provides short-term educational loans to help students cover school-related expenses when temporary financial difficulties arise. Our loan program is designed with student-friendly terms and a straightforward application process.

Loan Purposes:

  • Tuition and enrollment fees
  • Examination fees
  • Academic expenses
  • Other school-related expenses

Proposed Terms:

Monthly Interest: 3%
Service/Processing Fee: ₱150
Late Payment Penalty: 3%

Gadget Loans

WAIS Gadget Loans provide affordable financing for laptops, tablets, and other study devices with student-friendly terms. Members can access the technology they need for their education without the burden of high upfront costs.
